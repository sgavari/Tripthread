TripThread Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

TripThread is a group trip-planning app: create a trip, invite friends, chat, and split expenses together. This page explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address (email/password sign-in), or your name, email, and profile photo if you sign in with Google.
  • Profile information: the display name and photo associated with your account, plus any per-trip nickname you choose to set.
  • Trip content: trip names, destinations, dates, and a representative photo (automatically looked up from Wikipedia's public image API based on the destination you enter).
  • Messages: chat messages you send within a trip, visible to that trip's other members.
  • Expenses: expense descriptions, amounts, and who they're split among, visible to that trip's other members.
  • Push notification token: a device token used to deliver notifications about new messages, if you allow notifications.

How we use it

Everything collected is used only to operate the app's features: authenticating you, showing your trips, running group chat, splitting expenses, and notifying you about new messages. We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Who can see your data

Trip content, messages, and expenses are visible only to the members of that specific trip — people you've explicitly invited or who joined with your trip's invite code. Your account profile (name/photo) is visible to other TripThread users you share a trip with, so they can tell who's who.

Infrastructure

TripThread is built on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Messaging). Data is stored and processed on Google Cloud infrastructure, encrypted in transit and at rest, under Google's standard cloud security practices.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from within the app (Trips tab → account menu → Delete account). This permanently deletes your profile and removes you from every trip. Messages and expenses you already contributed remain visible to your trip-mates as part of that trip's shared history — the same way deleting your account on any group messaging app doesn't erase messages you already sent to a group.

Children's privacy

TripThread is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
